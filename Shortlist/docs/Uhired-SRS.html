Uhired — Software Requirements Specification

Version 1.0 · July 20, 2026 · Timeline: 30 days (1 month MVP)

Executive summary: Uhired is an AI-powered interview practice and hiring platform. The codebase already covers ~70–80% of MVP features. This SRS targets a production-ready launch in 4 weeks by hardening existing flows—not building from scratch.

1. Product Vision

Uhired enables candidates to practice realistic AI interviews and enables companies to run structured hiring workflows with invite-based access, AI-led sessions, automated scoring, recruiter dashboards, and shareable scorecards.

2. User Roles

ActorResponsibilities
Practice CandidatePurchase or redeem practice session, complete AI interview, receive scorecard
Company AdminCreate requirements, invite candidates, monitor sessions, share scorecards
Invited CandidateJoin interview via access code + email verification
Master AdminManage companies, promo codes, platform metrics

3. Major Modules

Public Website

Landing, about, contact, privacy, terms, CTAs

Practice Interviews

Domain selection, Razorpay payment, promo codes, AI interview

Company Hiring

Requirements, AI questions, bulk invites, dashboards

Live Interview

Realtime AI, camera/mic, transcript, timer, video (company)

Scoring

Scorecard, question grading, PDF export, share links

Master Admin

Companies, promo codes, practice monitoring

6. Out of Scope (Phase 2)

  • ATS/HRIS integrations (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday)
  • Native mobile apps
  • Multi-language support
  • Advanced multi-role RBAC
  • SOC2 certification

7. External Dependencies

  • OpenAI — Realtime interview + scoring
  • Razorpay — Practice payments
  • PostgreSQL — Database (Prisma)
  • SMTP/SES — Candidate invite emails
  • AWS S3 — Video storage

8. MVP Acceptance Criteria

  • Practice: purchase/promo → interview → scorecard works end-to-end
  • Company: requirement → invite → interview → dashboard with score
  • Master: company CRUD, promo codes, passcode regen
  • Share: expiring scorecard links + PDF download
